Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

+

Open the app, go to the login screen, and tap "Forgot Password?". Enter your registered email address, and we'll send you a reset link. Follow the instructions in the email to set a new password.

I'm not receiving my verification email

+

Check your spam or junk folder first. If you still don't see it, make sure you entered the correct email address. You can request a new verification email from the app. If the issue persists, contact our support team.

How do I update my profile information?

+

Open JOL Puzzles and navigate to Settings → Profile. From there you can update your display name, avatar, and other account details.

Can I use JOL Puzzles on multiple devices?

+

Yes! Simply log in with the same account on any supported device. Your progress, scores, and wallet balance will sync automatically across all your devices.

How does the referral system work?

+

Share your unique referral code with friends. When they sign up and join a game, both of you can earn rewards! Find your referral code in Settings → Referrals.
